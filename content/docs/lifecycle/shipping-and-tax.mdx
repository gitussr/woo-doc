Shipping and tax are two stages of the [cart totals
pipeline](/docs/lifecycle/cart#the-totals-calculation-pipeline). Both turn the
cart plus the customer's address into money added to the order. They are
grouped here because they interact — tax is calculated on shipping too.

### Adding a shipping method

Extend `WC_Shipping_Method`, implement `calculate_shipping( $package )` calling
`$this->add_rate( array( 'id' => …, 'label' => …, 'cost' => …, 'taxes' => … ) )`,
and register via `woocommerce_shipping_methods`.

### The calculation

<Lifecycle
  steps={[
    'For each line, fee, and the shipping total: find the product\'s tax class and status',

    'Rounding: the woocommerce_tax_round_at_subtotal option — "yes" sums untotalled tax then rounds once; "no" rounds each line then sums',

  ]}

/>

Rate matching is `WC_Tax::find_rates()` / `WC_Tax::get_matched_tax_rates()` —
filterable via `woocommerce_find_rates` and `woocommerce_matched_tax_rates`.

<Callout type="warn">
  **Prices entered with or without tax** (`woocommerce_prices_include_tax`) is
  a store-wide decision that changes how every price is interpreted. Combined
  with "display prices in shop/cart including or excluding tax", it is the
  source of most "the total is off by a cent" reports. Changing it on a live
  store re-interprets every existing price.
</Callout>

### Where tax display is decided

| Setting | Controls |
| --- | --- |
| Prices entered inclusive/exclusive | How stored prices are read |
| Display in shop | Whether catalog prices show inc/exc tax |
| Display during cart/checkout | Whether cart totals show inc/exc tax |
| Price display suffix | The "(incl. VAT)" style text after a price |
