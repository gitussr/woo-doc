Shipping & Tax

The shipping and tax subsystems — shipping zones, methods and packages, how rates are produced and filtered, then tax classes, rates, the calculation, and where inclusive/exclusive pricing and rounding are decided.

P1 · Core DevelopmentAudienceIntermediate → AdvancedStatusDraft · Step 1 foundationApplies toWordPress 7.1 · WooCommerce 11.0.1

Shipping and tax are two stages of the cart totals pipeline. Both turn the cart plus the customer's address into money added to the order. They are grouped here because they interact — tax is calculated on shipping too.

Shipping

Zones, methods, packages

Where does this come from?
What am I looking at?
A shipping option you want to hide for certain carts.
Where does it come from?
A WC_Shipping_Rate produced by a method instance in the matched zone, passed through woocommerce_package_rates.
What controls it?
The zone/method config; the woocommerce_package_rates filter; the method's own settings.
What can I safely modify?
Unset the rate in woocommerce_package_rates based on cart contents / customer; adjust free_shipping's minimum via its settings or woocommerce_shipping_free_shipping_is_available.
What should I avoid?
Hiding the rate only in the template — it is still selectable via the Store API and can be chosen.

Classic (PHP templates + hooks)

Rates render in the review-order table; choosing one posts via wc-ajax (update_shipping_method / update_order_review) and stores chosen_shipping_methods in the session.

Blocks (Cart/Checkout Blocks)

The Cart/Checkout Block shows rates from the Store API; POST wc/store/v1/cart/select-shipping-rate stores the choice. Same WC_Shipping packages and woocommerce_package_rates filter underneath.

Adding a shipping method

Extend WC_Shipping_Method, implement calculate_shipping( $package ) calling $this->add_rate( array( 'id' => …, 'label' => …, 'cost' => …, 'taxes' => … ) ), and register via woocommerce_shipping_methods.

Tax

Classes and rates

The calculation

Rate matching is WC_Tax::find_rates() / WC_Tax::get_matched_tax_rates() — filterable via woocommerce_find_rates and woocommerce_matched_tax_rates.

Prices entered with or without tax (woocommerce_prices_include_tax) is a store-wide decision that changes how every price is interpreted. Combined with "display prices in shop/cart including or excluding tax", it is the source of most "the total is off by a cent" reports. Changing it on a live store re-interprets every existing price.
